Artificial Intelligence

CS650 is a general introduction to Artificial Intelligence. The course examines computer systems that exhibit traits associated with human intelligence, including understanding language, reasoning about the visual environment, solving complex problems, and engaging with current developments in the field.

How we will learn

We will connect AI theory to worked problems, algorithm traces, code, and practical applications. The goal is to understand not just what an AI method does, but why it works, when it is appropriate, and what its limitations are.

The syllabus emphasizes knowledge representation, problem solving, learning, intelligent-agent design, reasoning, vision, language, robotics, medicine, and computational design.

Course Overview

What CS650 is about

The course begins with knowledge-representation formalisms and search techniques and expands into natural language processing, computer vision, logic and theorem proving, game playing, inheritance networks, neural networks, genetic algorithms, expert systems, planning, uncertainty, machine learning, and robotics.

Course Learning Objectives

What you should be able to do

  • Explain the basic knowledge-representation, problem-solving, and learning methods of Artificial Intelligence.
  • Assess the applicability, strengths, and weaknesses of those methods for particular engineering problems.
  • Develop intelligent systems by assembling solutions to concrete computational problems.
  • Understand the role of knowledge representation, problem solving, and learning in intelligent-system engineering.
  • Develop sufficient interest and foundation to pursue more advanced subjects in Artificial Intelligence.
